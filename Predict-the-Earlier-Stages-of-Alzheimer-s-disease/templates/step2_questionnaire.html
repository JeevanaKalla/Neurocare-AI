🧠 Step 2: Cognitive Screening Assessment

This module evaluates memory, orientation, language, and daily functioning to detect early cognitive decline patterns.

📌 Workflow: Biomarkers → Cognitive Assessment → AI Diagnosis Report
Patient Questionnaire
🧠 What This Test Measures
  • Memory Recall: Ability to remember recent events
  • Orientation: Awareness of time and place
  • Language Function: Word-finding ability
  • Executive Function: Daily task management ability
📊 Clinical Importance

Cognitive screening helps detect early signs of Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI), which may progress to Alzheimer’s disease if untreated.

⚠️ Clinical Note

This assessment is a screening tool and not a diagnostic test. Clinical evaluation by a neurologist is required for confirmation.
